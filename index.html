HOW TO PLAY

Notice. Question. Connect.

MOVE

W walks forward along the path you face. A / D turn onto side paths. S steps back. Move the mouse to look around. Path labels on screen can also be clicked.

NOTICE

Look at something for a moment to notice it. When a ring appears, press E to look closer. Tell your team what you found.

BORROW A SENSE

1 Human · 2 Bird sees from above · 3 Frog feels the water · 4 Leopard reads tracks. Each clue needs the right sense.

THE LOOP

A day lasts eight minutes. If the pangolin is not home by sunset, the day simply starts again, and you keep what you learned.

THE INSTALLATION · ENTRANCE

Leave your phone at the door.
The forest begins.

In the physical installation, visitors pass a moss-lined corridor and step through a living portal into the forest. Real plants, moss, mist and projected light surround them; the digital layer is sound, light and time. This prototype is that forest, on screen.

OBSERVATION

MYSTERY

SELF-DISCOVERY

DAY & TIME · C

Choose a day and a time.

The installation runs on a fixed loop. In this digital prototype you can jump between days and times to explore how the forest changes from dawn to last light.
