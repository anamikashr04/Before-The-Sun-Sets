HOW TO PLAY

Notice. Question. Connect.

MOVE

W walks forward along the path you face. A / D turn onto side paths. S steps back. Move the mouse to look around. Path labels on screen can also be clicked.

NOTICE

Hold your gaze on something for a moment to notice it and earn points. When the ring appears, press E to inspect.

BORROW A SENSE

1 Human · 2 Bird sees from above · 3 Frog feels the water · 4 Leopard reads tracks. Each clue needs the right sense. Senses drain energy.

THE LOOP

A day lasts eight minutes. If the sun sets before the pangolin is safe, the day begins again. Your journal keeps every clue.
